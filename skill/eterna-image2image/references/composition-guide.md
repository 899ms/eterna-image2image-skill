# Cinematic Composition Guide / 电影构图指南

Use this reference when the task asks for cinematic framing, stronger film language, scene blocking, or reduced AI-generated visual habits.

当任务要求更强电影感、镜头语言、场面调度，或减少 AI 生成味时，使用此参考。

## Core Intent / 核心意图

The frame should feel like a still extracted from a larger story. Prefer motivated composition over decorative composition.

画面应该像从一个更大故事中截取出来的一帧。优先选择有动机的构图，而不是装饰性构图。

## Framing Rules / 取景规则

- Avoid default centered portrait framing unless the user asks for a poster, ID card, or symmetrical shot.
- Use off-center subjects, over-the-shoulder shapes, partial foreground occlusion, doorframes, windows, mirrors, curtains, cars, or furniture to create depth.
- Let the environment tell part of the story: weather, room layout, street geometry, practical lights, props, and distance between subjects.
- Keep horizon and perspective plausible. Avoid impossible room geometry, warped streets, or floating objects.
- Use negative space to express isolation, tension, longing, danger, scale, or silence.

- 除非用户要求海报、身份卡或对称镜头，否则避免默认居中肖像构图。
- 使用偏离中心的主体、过肩形态、局部前景遮挡、门框、窗户、镜子、窗帘、汽车或家具建立空间层次。
- 让环境承担叙事：天气、房间布局、街道几何、实景光源、道具和人物距离。
- 保持地平线和透视可信，避免不可能的房间结构、扭曲街道或悬浮物体。
- 用负空间表达孤独、紧张、渴望、危险、尺度或沉默。

## Lens And Camera Language / 镜头语言

- Use natural focal-length language: 28-35mm for environmental intimacy, 40-50mm for natural drama, 70-100mm for compressed portraits.
- Prefer eye-level or slightly low/high motivated camera angles; avoid arbitrary drone-like perspectives.
- For intimate scenes, use close framing, shallow but believable depth of field, and practical foreground softness.
- For night streets or war/drama scenes, let haze, rain, smoke, glass, or atmosphere shape depth without overdoing effects.

- 使用自然焦段语言：28-35mm 适合环境亲密感，40-50mm 适合自然剧情，70-100mm 适合压缩感肖像。
- 优先使用平视或有动机的轻微仰/俯角，避免随意的航拍式视角。
- 亲密场景使用较近景别、浅但可信的景深，以及实景前景虚化。
- 夜街、战争或剧情场景可用雾气、雨、烟、玻璃或空气感塑造深度，但不要过度特效化。

## Lighting Motivation / 光线动机

- Every strong light should have a believable source: window, lamp, streetlight, fire, screen, shop sign, car headlight, moon, overcast sky.
- Keep highlights soft and controlled. ETERNA-inspired images should not feel like HDR product photography.
- Use backlight or rim light when it supports story separation, not as a generic beauty effect.
- In low light, keep shadow shapes readable instead of crushing them into black blobs.

- 每个强光都应有可信来源：窗户、台灯、路灯、火光、屏幕、店招、车灯、月光或阴天。
- 高光柔和且受控。ETERNA 启发图像不应像 HDR 产品摄影。
- 当逆光或轮廓光有助于叙事分离时使用，不要把它当作通用美化效果。
- 低照度场景中，暗部形状应可读，而不是压成黑块。

## Anti-AI Habits / 降低 AI 味

Avoid:

- Overly symmetrical centered subjects with no narrative reason.
- Perfectly clean skin, fabric, walls, or streets.
- Unmotivated lens flare, random bokeh balls, fake film scratches, or heavy halation.
- Too many visible signs, text fragments, logos, labels, or unreadable typography.
- Hands or props placed only to "look cinematic" without story logic.
- Poster-like glamour lighting when the user asked for a film still.

避免：

- 没有叙事理由的过度对称和居中主体。
- 过于干净的皮肤、布料、墙面或街道。
- 无动机眩光、随机散景球、假胶片划痕或重度光晕。
- 过多招牌、文字碎片、logo、标签或不可读字体。
- 只为"看起来电影感"而缺乏故事逻辑的手势或道具。
- 用户要求电影截图时，却生成海报式美光。
